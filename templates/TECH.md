> localized work — a tech spec for a one-file fix is waste.

## Approach
The chosen design, in a few sentences. What changes and where.

## Key interfaces / data shapes
The APIs, schemas, types, or events this introduces or alters.

## Alternatives considered
- **<alternative>** — why not (the reason matters more than the list).

## Risks & mitigations

## Migration / rollback

What will prove it works: unit, integration, and the verification evidence the
ship gate will see.
